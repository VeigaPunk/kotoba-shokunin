# QUALITY — kotoba's admission bar

A candidate clears this bar only when every applicable criterion holds. Criteria are
append-only: a superseding row names the row it replaces in `supersedes`; rows are never
rewritten. No criterion exists without a lesson behind it — `grounded in` names the class
row in `corpus/lessons/round-46.md` § Mistakes. Tier definitions live in
`corpus/SOURCES.md` (SOURCES contract v1 rule 3: no second method — claim admission stays
here, the research method stays chousa's).
