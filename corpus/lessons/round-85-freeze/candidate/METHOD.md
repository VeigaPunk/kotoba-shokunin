# Preparatory craft method: compress without making the next decision unknowable

Status: author draft, not an admitted improvement or frozen numbered plan. No baseline answers or private rubric/holdouts consulted. This is a synthesis for human review, not a claim about model performance. Existing round74 already teaches conditional loading; the proposed increment is a repeatable audit of whether that condition can be decided in time.

## Decision procedure

1. Mark each instruction's decision and the earliest action it constrains. Extract its subject/state, prerequisite, timing, exception, stop or escalation, and authority/version qualifier. A sentence that appears descriptive may establish one of these. If the source is ambiguous or inconsistent, record the ambiguity and obtain its owner's ruling rather than compressing it into a newly invented rule.
2. For each proposed reference, name its trigger and identify the evidence available BEFORE the constrained action without opening that reference. Trace prerequisites of that evidence recursively until they terminate in the request, an already-visible rule, or an authorized observation. A trigger that requires the withheld classifier is circular. A signal observed only after the protected action is too late. Keep a small classifier or mandatory pre-action read in the body; do not move the obligation merely because its full explanation is long.
3. Decide among three destinations: KEEP the minimum decision/stop text visible; MOVE detail only behind a timely independently observable trigger, with its direct root-relative pointer and unreadable/unknown fallback; DELETE only a true duplicate with a named retained occurrence that has the same scope, authority and temporal force. Repeated text at different execution entrypoints is not redundant if either entrypoint can be reached alone. Do not treat unknown as false. Missing optional detail can take a stated safe default; missing required policy must block that affected action, not unrelated work.
4. Separate the need-to-read rule from the material-to-read. Preserve the guard in the body even if the reference supplies calculations, examples or a long classification table. For an unresolved operation class, an unconditional read of that one classifier before action can be appropriate; that does not justify loading every reference on every task. If no lawful observation can resolve the class, stop that action and ask its owner. Do not invent permissions, environment facts or domain policy.
5. Replay the original and compressed wording at each relevant state: normal, exception, missing/unknown evidence, and failure or uncertain outcome. Write the next permitted action and the forbidden transition for each. Compare prerequisite reachability, before/after ordering, exact stop/escalation boundary, state/version/scope, and conditional detail. A newly permitted action, removed exception, or later guard is meaning loss. A corrected defective original is a separately disclosed repair, not equivalent compression.
6. Compare economy only after that equivalence check. Name what was removed, why the retained text suffices, and which detail remains conditionally unloaded. Counts measure size only. File existence and repeated keywords do not validate meaning. If the shorter text fails, retain the longer rule or redesign the split. Review case-specific differences against baseline; an already-correct baseline is a tie, never a manufactured win.

## Distinguishing examples

- A nonzero status is an observable trigger for error-message explanations; it cannot trigger a completeness check required even after status zero. Put acceptance's integrity guard before acceptance, and defer only failure diagnosis.
- A timeout after sending is not proof of failure. Preserve reconciliation before another mutation and the stop when outcome cannot be determined. This is preservation of a supplied case rule, not creation of a general retry policy.
- A version qualifier and a sent/draft exception are small but decision-bearing. Removing either is not deduplication. Retain both even if the line count rises.
