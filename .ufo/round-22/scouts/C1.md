file** it never names: `verifiers/corpus-baseline.json` (re-pin `tracked_files`, plus `report_total`
for the Sources option). Precedent exists — round 16 re-recorded the baseline (`tracked_files 45→46,
report_total 30→32`, `SCORECARD.md:32`) — so the act is legitimate; the **claim text** is what is
