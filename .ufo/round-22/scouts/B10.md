## Defects named

**D1 — `verifiers/verify_corpus_integrity.py:350` (with `:489`) — a repo-root-relative report
pointer goes dead when moved into a corpus file.** `base = Path(rel).parent` and the injected
`pointer_exists` is `resolve_pointer(_repo / base, token)`, i.e. relative report pointers
resolve from the *citing file's own directory*, while `verify_skill_checklist.py`'s
`citation_resolve` resolves the same token from the *repo root*. Evidence:
`corpus/knowledge/evidence-base.md:154: ../.ufo/reports/round-7/kotoba.md` reported dead in the
mirror (step 10) while the identical token passes in `SKILL.md`; normalizing to host-absolute
form makes `report_dead=0/36` (step 11). Consequence for claim B: "verbatim bytes" cannot be
literal for base-relative pointers — the move carries exactly one documented, content-preserving
adaptation (same class as the round-13 `shokunin:`-prefix repair).

**D2 — stale check count: `SKILL.md:194` (`**27 checks**`), `SKILL.md:541` (`27/27 checks`),
`SKILL.md:546` (`27 checks`) vs a 28-check instrument.** Evidence:
`python3 verifiers/verify_harness_claims.py` → `28/28 checks pass` (live and mirror);
`verifiers/baseline.json` `checks` has 28 keys, the extra ones being `oc.log.skills_rescan_max`
and `oc.roots.plugins_cache`, introduced by round 18 (`02302c5`, whose subject says "re-census
against moved substrate — omp ff59fc1b, opencode v2.0.26"). Two of the three stale sites
(:541, :546) lie **inside the moved bytes**, so an unqualified verbatim move carries a false
count into the corpus file. Recommend: keep the moved bytes verbatim, record the errata in the
new file's header, and correct `:194` as a separate one-line fix (not required for B; the
line count stays 456 either way).

**D3 — the move is not gate-neutral for the corpus instrument until the baseline is
re-recorded: `verifiers/corpus-baseline.json`.** Evidence (step 10): `DRIFT tracked_files
observed=53 expected=52`, `DRIFT report_total observed=36 expected=34`; green at 9/9 after the
re-record (step 12). Naming it prevents the integration from reading as "the move broke the
corpus gate".

**D4 (minor, observed) — `corpus/README.md` row count arithmetic.** The row I drafted states
`601 → 456`; if the integrator also lands the D2 fix at `:194` (a same-line-length edit), the
count is unchanged, but any further edit to the moved region must re-derive the number from
