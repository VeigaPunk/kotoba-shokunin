## Findings

**F1 — the recorded 41 is CONFIRMED; zero drift.** Box count at HEAD `b16300f` is 41 across
`15/9/9/8`, by six mutually independent instruments: (a) the printed awk re-run from the file's own
extracted bytes (#3), (b) `grep -c '^- \[ \]'` on the HEAD blob and on the worktree (#6, #7), (c) an
independent awk total (#5), (d) an explicit line-range recount that does not depend on any header
text (#12), (e) `verifiers/checklist-baseline.json`'s 41 mapped rows with the identical section multiset
(#17), (f) the live gate's `PASS checklist.box_map_complete: evaluated 41` (#18). The worktree file is
byte-identical to the HEAD blob (#9), so the two counts cannot diverge. Prose sites `SCORECARD.md:28`
("41 boxes total, 15/9/9/8") and `SHONEN.md:15` ("15/9/9/8 = 41 boxes") are therefore correct at HEAD.

**F2 — the one apparent divergence is correctly attributed, not stale.** `SHONEN.md:15` embeds the
sample output `## Trigger-fidelity checklist: 14; …` while a HEAD re-run emits `15`. The sentence binds
that output to the parenthetical immediately preceding it — `was 14/9/9/8 = 40: <cmd> → <output>` — and
at `0089974~1` (the commit before round 15's landing commit `0089974`, "round-15: the SKILL.md checklists
become executable") the printed awk emits exactly `14; 9; 9; 8` and `grep -c` gives `40` (#20), byte-for-byte.
So the embed is a reproducible pre-state witness, not a false claim. Round-21 scout G4 recorded it as a
defect (`.ufo/round-21/scouts/G4.md:49`, "quoted sample output stale … not reproducible as printed");
that reading is a mis-read of the attribution. What is genuinely missing is a pin: the embed names no
commit, so a reader who re-runs at HEAD gets 15 and only the surrounding prose tells them why —
G4's mis-read is exactly the failure this invites. Advisory for the round-22 B lane: SHONEN.md is not
SKILL.md, and box 1's claim is the 41, not the embed, so this does not gate promotion.

`- [~]` × 0, any-marker × 41 (#10); all 41 markers sit on canonical `- [ ]`-prefixed lines (#15) and
each line carries exactly one marker (#16). There are no indented, nested, alternate-marker, or inline
boxes, and no boxes anywhere outside the four checklist sections (#14: the other nine sections count 0),
