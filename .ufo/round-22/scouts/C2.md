### F3. When the nudge would fire if no capability lands

- The firing rule (`AXES.md:66-73`) needs **both** conditions at a round's
  start: (i) every owned axis at/above the line 10.0, and (ii) no capability
  within the window. The fleet's worked example reads (ii) as window
  completion, not partial: kaidoku:771-772 "the stagnation nudge does not fire
  before N=10"; kaidoku:1272 records the completed state ("10/10 — window
  complete").
- Window completion: end of the 3rd capability-free counted lane-round → **end
  of round 23** (count-from-21) or **end of round 24** (count-from-22).
  Earliest nudge-eligible round: **round 24** or **round 25**.
- Condition (i) is false and does not change under the scenario: depth is 9
  below the 10.0 line — the declaration itself records "the nudge is dormant"
  (`SCORECARD.md:136-139`) — and round 22 has no depth claim. **So if no
  capability lands and no depth band-10 move lands, the nudge never fires: the
  window completes as bookkeeping and the line is the blocker.** If a depth
  band-10 move lands in round R, the nudge fires for the first round after both
  events: max(24, R+1) primary / max(25, R+1) under the LOG reading.
- Condition (ii) is vacuous while the table has never held a row (defect 2):
  for any N, "no capability has entered … within the window" holds. The count
  is non-binding until a capability row exists; the line is the only live
  switch. (Carried from `.ufo/round-21/scouts/C2.md` defect 3; re-verified at
  HEAD.)

## Defects named

1. **Two records, two counting starts — `SCORECARD.md:126-128` vs
   `LOG.md:1213`.** SCORECARD: "the clock starts at the declaration round"
   (round 21 counted; count = 1/3 now). LOG: "prospective from round 22"
   (count = 0/3 now), and the round-22 dispatch repeats the LOG reading.
   One-round offset in every consequence: window complete end of 23 vs 24;
   first nudge-eligible round 24 vs 25. Evidence: commands 8-10 (both verbatim
   strings); only one reading is right and the artifact of record is the
   SCORECARD `saturation` field (`AXES.md:82`).
2. **`AXES.md:68-70` condition (2) is vacuous for this lane.** The table has
   never held a row (`SCORECARD.md:120`, verified at HEAD), so "no capability
   has entered the table within the lane's stagnation window" holds for any N;
   the window count cannot fire anything by itself and the line (depth ≥ 10.0)
   is the only live switch. Carried from `.ufo/round-21/scouts/C2.md` defect 3;
   unchanged at HEAD.
3. **Firing semantics undefined — N as lookback cap vs mandatory wait.** The
   declaration embeds both: "The waiver applies from the round after this
   recording (round 22)" (`SCORECARD.md:151-152`) reads as firing/eligibility
   from 22; the fleet's worked example reads "does not fire before N"
   (kaidoku:771-772) — a mandatory wait — and the declaration's rationale
   treats the window as a device to fill ("the observed no-move run is exactly
   three", `SCORECARD.md:142-145`). `AXES.md:66-73` does not define which.
4. **No current-count state exists in the record.** The declaration states the
   rule, not the running count; the round-22 report skeleton
   (`.ufo/reports/round-22/kotoba.md`, 59 lines) has no bookkeeping field, and
   SCORECARD §Saturation carries no count. The window position is reconstructible
   only by interpretation — exactly why defects 1 and 3 bite — and each future
   round must restate it or it goes stale.
