## Trust boundary and exclusions

The checks establish consistency among copies in a producer-shaped JSON document; they do **not** prove provenance. Without a signature or trusted producer receipt, a caller that coherently rewrites rows, mirrors, and summary can still fabricate the document. Legacy row-only payload shapes also remain accepted; this change does not claim to authenticate arbitrary caller-authored JSON.

P2 did not change or claim the concurrent raw duplicate-member, scope-file resolution, or scoped-out metadata work. The shared worktree contains those other seat changes; retain their ownership separately. No Git index, commit, or ref operation was performed.
