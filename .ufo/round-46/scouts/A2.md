## What the committed declaration constrains

- **Membership and status:** the payload may scope out only an ID present in the selected scope file and its own independent payload; the record must say `SCOPED_OUT`, and the payload's `scoped_out` reason must be non-empty (`compare-1101213a.py:647-695, 911-966`). A payload cannot add an unlisted row to the default committed set.
- **Scope-entry kind and reason:** `load_scope_file()` requires each entry to be an object, its `kind` to be in the known enum, and its committed `reason` to be a non-empty string (`:784-821`). The parsed entry is used as a membership set; the entry's kind and reason are not subsequently compared with payload metadata. The probe kind is a separate field: it is compared to `probe_contract.kind`, and the hard-coded recomputation accepts `rule-spread-census` (`:823-849, 953-977`).
- **Payload reason and evidence:** `parse_scoped_out()` validates only that the payload reason is non-empty; it does not bind it to the committed reason. No evidence field exists in the committed per-row entries (`harness-independent-scope.json:13-29`), and scoped-row `evidence` text is not validated against the committed file.
- **Probe target:** the committed `probe_contract` names a kind and a prose census rule, but no path, target digest, or target allow-list (`harness-independent-scope.json:7-11`). The payload supplies the target. The driver requires an absolute path to an existing directory, then recomputes over that directory (`compare-1101213a.py:845-908`); no comparison to a committed target is made.
- **Probe result:** this is checked. The declared `observable` must equal the recomputed observable for the selected target; empty/no-block carriers and probes where all five rules agree fail as false blockers (`:968-989`). Other prose fields (`claim`, `falsified_by`) are not validated as evidence of the reason.
