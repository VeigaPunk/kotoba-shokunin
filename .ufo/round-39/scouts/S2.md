## DEFECTS

**Mine (found and fixed in this seat, none left open):**
1. I first mis-located the artifacts (assumed the brief's paths) and mis-attributed
   `v6-oracle.py`'s default resolution as broken — its `parents[2]` is *correct* for
   `evidence/`; my draft copy at a different depth was what failed. Corrected in F0/F4.
2. I ran a `--json` flag against `v5-derive_floors.py`, which has no such option
   (exit 2); that was my error, not the script's. Re-ran bare.
3. **Most important:** I initially measured "runs as committed" with `--repo .` typed by
   hand, which masks the breakage on 4 scripts — including r38's own C3 replay
   instrument. My first draft of this report therefore claimed 4 artifacts green when
   only 2 are, and cleared C3 unconditionally. Caught on final re-verification by
   re-running everything bare; F2/F3 and the C3 verdict are corrected.

**Others (all pre-existing, reported not fixed):**
1. **Curator / integrator (HIGH, r37 carried into r38):** the r37 prune moved 5 scripts
   without repairing paths; r38 fixed 2 (R1) and left 3. `parents[]` counting is the
   systemic root cause — the fix idiom already exists in the repo (`_repo_root()` in
   `R1-counter_blind.py`), so the repair is mechanical.
2. **Integrator (HIGH):** the r37 prune deleted `shapes/` and `fixtures/oc-log-mutant.log`
   while keeping the scripts that read them, without recording their regeneration recipe.
3. **Integrator (HIGH, C3):** r38's `v5-verify_battery_cmark.py` — C3's own replay
   instrument — fails bare (`parents[3]`). r38 recorded "both replays green"; that holds
   only with `--repo .` supplied by hand. Either put the flag in the documented usage or
   apply the walk fix.
4. **Integrator (MEDIUM):** `v7-c3_landing_proof.py` banked with 3 absent inputs (F5).
5. **Integrator (LOW):** r38's own evidence dir is not self-contained (F3d) — its scripts
   import modules that only exist in the r37 dir.
6. **R1 (LOW):** the blind-tree scrub's enumerated list is stale by construction; a
   scan-to-fixpoint sweep is strictly better (F4).
