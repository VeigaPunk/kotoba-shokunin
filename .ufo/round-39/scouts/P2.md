### F1. The bug, at source (red-before, both grammars)

`verifiers/verify_harness_independent.py:451` (pre-fix) read
`sum(1 for v in init_counts.values() if v > 1)` over a `run=`-keyed dict — "runs
carrying more than one init-count line". The id names **decreases between consecutive
counts**; the pin note (`baseline.json:130`) says "max of `message=init count=N`". So
the population *and* the direction were both wrong. HAR's extractor
(`verify_harness_claims.py:460-492`) is a file-order `re.findall` with `a->b` strings.

```
$ for m in mut-hargrammar-inc mut-hargrammar-dec mut-quoted mut-hargrammar-crossrun; do
    HAR: verify_harness_claims.py --oc-log $m.log --no-baseline --json
    IND: verify_harness_independent.py --oc-log $m.log --json ; done
```
BEFORE (`.ufo/round-39/drafts/p2/before-mutants.txt`):
```
mut-hargrammar-inc   HAR max=2 viol=[]          IND max=2 viol=1        <-- 1 where the id requires []
mut-hargrammar-dec   HAR max=3 viol=['3->2','2->1']  IND max=3 viol=1   <-- count instead of strings
mut-quoted           HAR max=0 viol=[]          IND max=2 viol=1        <-- IND counts a non-grammar form
mut-hargrammar-crossrun HAR max=2 viol=['2->1'] IND max=1 viol=0        <-- IND keys by run, HAR does not
```
AFTER (`.ufo/round-39/drafts/p2/FINAL-arms.txt`, at IND sha `872993f8`):
```
mut-hargrammar-inc      HAR max=2 viol=[]              IND max=2 viol=[]
mut-hargrammar-dec      HAR max=3 viol=['3->2','2->1'] IND max=3 viol=['3->2','2->1']
mut-quoted              HAR max=0 viol=[]              IND max=0 viol=[]
mut-hargrammar-crossrun HAR max=2 viol=['2->1']        IND max=2 viol=['2->1']
```
All four legs now match HAR exactly. The predicate lives at
`verifiers/verify_harness_independent.py:577` (`if init_values[i] < init_values[i - 1]`).
