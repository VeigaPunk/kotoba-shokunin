### Key findings

1. **The round-4 lesson is the durable vector for two counts its own round's
   audit falsified.** `round-4.md:9-11` repeats "13 identical, 4 absent, 1
   doubled"; `round-4-aggregator-audit.md:29-38` re-ran the pipeline and got
   11 identical + 4 empty + 3 distinct, and `agent-def-audit-round6.md:64`
   later recorded 14/18. `round-4.md:16-18` repeats the "3–4-line round
   family"; the audit (`:47-49`) named the 6-line `aggregator-round-4.md`
   exception, verified live here. The lesson never records either correction,
   even though the audit was committed in the same round (report round-4:41).
2. **Host drift makes every round-1/round-4 line anchor dead at HEAD.**
   `bailian-*`, `sekhmet-l3`, `xbrd-ask` are gone; `~/.config/opencode/agents/`
   and `command/` are gone (dir now holds 3 JSON files); `wwkd` moved to
   `~/.kimi-code/skills/wwkd/SKILL.md` and is 135 lines (was 233). Only the
   omarchy anchor (`round-1-aggregator-audit.md:43` → live L10) survives.
3. **Both corpus instruments exit 0 on all five files.** The defects above
   are invisible to the gates: the integrity verifier counts 32 report
   pointers and 27 commits with 0 dead — it never resolves the tilde
   skill-file paths (only 1 tilde pointer is counted at all). "Gate-green"
   does not mean "claim-true" for lesson content.
4. **The round-1 audit's own conformance claim is false for its round.**
   `round-1-aggregator-audit.md:18` says "no dangling index paths", but
   `README.md@1d93182` pointed at `../.ufo/reports/round-1/kotoba.md` — from
   `corpus/` that resolves inside `kotoba-shokunin/.ufo/`, which held no
   `reports/` at all (`git ls-tree` empty). Round 11's verifier later caught
   this class ("9 dead `../.ufo/…` rows", `LOG.md` round-11 entry).
5. **Defect-count drift across the audit family**: 3 (`round-1-aggregator-audit.md:15`,
   `round-5.md:25`) vs 4 (`round-1-aggregator-audit.md:53-74`,
   `round-4-aggregator-audit.md:65` "All four").
6. **Report-side arithmetic**: round-1 report:7 "20-line shonen seed" — the
   seed (`43e0c02:SKILL.md`) is **13** lines; round-4 report:16 "270 lines" —
