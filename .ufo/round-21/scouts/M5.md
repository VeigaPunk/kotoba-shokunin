## Findings

1. **Probe reproduces — non-vacuity holds at HEAD.** Deleting the OC-1 anchor
   substrings from a copy of the live v2.0.26 dump flips
   `surfaces.opencode_extract` from PASS (exit 0, 5/5 classes from 6/6 rules)
   to FAIL (exit 1) naming the orphaned class and the unmatched rule. The
   round-14 idiom (`--opencode-strings <copy> --check surfaces.opencode_extract`)
   works verbatim at HEAD, and the check is red-probeable against the real
   bundle, not only against the synthetic fixture (commands 11–12). The
   fixture-level variant (commands 13–14) is the same FAIL, exit 1.

2. **The FAIL names ONE id, not the three `instructions/` ids the slice
   expected.** At HEAD OC-1 owns `opencode:project/AGENTS.md`
   (`verifiers/verify_surfaces.py:398`), and the live baseline declares exactly
   five opencode ids: `opencode:project/AGENTS.md`,
   `opencode:config/AGENTS.md`, `opencode:read-hook/AGENTS.md`,
   `opencode:api-entries/instructions`, `opencode:config/instructions`
   (`verifiers/surfaces-baseline.json:245,252,259,266,273`). The three round-14
   names (`opencode:instructions/{AGENTS,CLAUDE,CONTEXT}.md`) were retired by
   the round-18 re-anchor and are recorded as historical, not live: census § B
   errata — "superseded-revision claims preserved as historical, not live
   classes" (`corpus/knowledge/instruction-surface-census-round13.md:164-175`,
   the id list at :172) — and the baseline `assembled` note — "the 9 round-13
   ids are historical" (`verifiers/surfaces-baseline.json:3`). The round-14-era
   expectation ("the FAIL naming the three `instructions/` ids",
   `SCORECARD.md:30`; probe row `corpus/knowledge/opencode-extraction-round14.md:109`)
   is therefore a stale *name* expectation for a round-21 probe: a reviewer
   holding it would misread this correct FAIL as a wrong one. The probe's
   substance (exit 1, `declared opencode class(es) produced by no rule`, OC-1
   named unmatched with its two missing anchors) is intact.

3. **Instrument division.** The OC-1 literal (`AGENTS.md`) survives in other
   anchors (`a$="AGENTS.md"`), so `surfaces.literal_present` does not see the
   OC-1 anchor deletion (command 15: zero opencode entries in its failure
   list); only `surfaces.opencode_extract` catches it. The opencode half's
   non-vacuity rests on the rule anchors, not on the literal probe.

4. **Corroboration, not new: the live surfaces gate is RED at HEAD (exit 1),
   entirely on the omp side.** `literal_present` misses 8/8 omp loader-group
   call shapes because the live omp binary moved `ff59fc1b…` → `bf1e86a9…`
   (`omp/18.11.1`) while the callee allowlist still names the round-18 minified
   callees (command 5). Named already this round by scouts F1/F5
   (`.ufo/round-21/scouts/F1.md`, `F5.md`); my run reproduces it. The opencode
   side is green at HEAD (5/5 from 6/6).

5. **Freshness pins.** The opencode binary sha256 still equals the round-18 pin
   `d9123b9b…` (v2.0.26), so the opencode half of
   `verifiers/baseline.json` `measured_against` is current; the omp half is
