## Open doors and handoff

- Close the **representation choice**, not just one alias: reject competing `checks`/`expected`/`observations` map forms or reconcile every published form. Otherwise a correct preferred map can mask a conflicting sibling map. The frozen parser's row-channel path reads `checks` before its fallback map precedence is considered.
- Enforce the committed scope boundary after parsing: a caller-supplied map must not turn any committed scope-out id into a derived/joined row, and a map key set must not enlarge the honest derived set. The complete-map successes above are direct evidence for C1's top-reachability target.
- Keep negative tests: scope-only map, scope-only map plus matching scope declaration/probes, non-dotted flat/nested keys, and all-`NOT-DERIVED` values. The last currently exits 1 rather than class-named exit 2; it is not a passing bypass, but the value/status distinction remains implicit for map forms.
- The probe did not rerun the full 46-arm r41 census, comparator self-test, or floors guard; no verifier, fixture, registry, or producer source was changed by X1. The worktree comparator and LOG were concurrently modified by other round-43 work; those changes are not attributed to this seat, and the in-flight comparator was deliberately not assessed.
