The receipt records, at input-read time and **before** the join:
- per side: `sha256`, `bytes`, `source` (`stdout-capture` | `file:<path>`), `exit`, `cmd`;
- for file-consumed sides: `file_sha256` + `file_bytes` (the file's own digest);
- `instrument_files`: sha256 of both instruments' bytes;
- `driver_argv`, `driver_cwd`, `head`, `recorded_at`;
- the join verdict: headline column, joined/scope/live counts, scoped-out map with blockers,
  disagreement count, exit + reason;
- a `mode` field: `frozen-inputs` (both sides consumed from files) vs `live-capture`.
