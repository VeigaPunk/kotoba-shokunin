**D-A — `oc.log.init_count_monotone` is filed as "D5, cosmetic ([] vs 0)" and as
"zero-delta representation row".** The readings are not two encodings of one measurement;
they are two *different predicates* for one id. IND (`verify_harness_independent.py:452`)

wrong predicate: at `:996-1000` it appends `value=1` then `value=2` and asserts
`oc.log.init_count_monotone == 1` — a fixture that restates the bug, so the green
`0 failure(s) over 86 assertions` certifies it rather than catching it. Live delta is 0 and
both sides read 0, so nothing is red today; the fixture is the whole proof (protocol §5).

**D-B — `oc.roots.plugins_cache` is filed as "D5, cosmetic (False vs 0)".** The two sides
read **different paths**: HAR (`verify_harness_claims.py:511`) tests
`HOME/.claude/plugins/cache`; IND (`verify_harness_independent.py:548`) counts skill-bearing
entries under `oc_config.parent.parent/.cache/opencode`. Live: HAR's path is **absent**, IND's
