   (`3:5-4:0` means "to the start of line 4") — first run reported 7 mismatches,
   2 of which were my parser's artifact. Fixed to end at col 0 → line-1.
5. The mutant runner initially could not see V4's `_closer_indent_ok` shape nor
   the landed regex-carried cap; the first "unpinned" verdict was re-derived after
   adding those patterns (the conclusion held for the landed shape).
6. My before-side byte-invariance receipts initially carried an appended `rc=`
   line, so `cmp` compared unequal-length files; re-captured clean before use.
7. `mutants.py` has no `--tag` argument; I passed one once and the run errored out
   before doing anything (no artifact written). Trivial, recorded.
8. Two checks used transient scratch outside the lane repo (`/tmp/p7-nb.txt`, a
