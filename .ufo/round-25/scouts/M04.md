## Findings

The red probe passes for both pin kinds tested. The unmodified control baseline holds all three pins; replacing the SHA pin with 64 zeroes or the mtime with `2000-01-01T00:00:00.000000000+00:00` makes the corresponding pin `DRIFT` and exits 1. JSON mode agrees on the SHA corruption. The guard maps `DRIFT` to 1 at `verifiers/check_substrate_pins.py:76-85`, returns the highest verdict code at `:88-90`, and compares SHA/mtime at `:177-195`.
