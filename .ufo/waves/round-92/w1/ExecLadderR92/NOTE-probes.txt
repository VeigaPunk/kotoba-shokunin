regular-file readings are the corrected ones in candidate-work-root.txt (rc=2, COULD-NOT-EVALUATE
--work <path>: not a directory, zero stderr bytes, zero tracebacks), taken after recreating the
blocker as a regular file.

the round-92 base defect (crash / accepted duplicate / uncompared 127), which the candidate no
longer exhibits. Their printed lines show the new readings (rc=2 named; rc=3 named; rc=3 named).
