## Headline clauses that printed lines do not turn on (Q26)

“what the `allowed-tools` frontmatter field **means** in **each client** that **documents it**” carries at least three substantive clauses: (1) page states/documentation status, (2) semantic meaning, (3) per-client coverage. The printed headline line only counts rows satisfying structural/lexical snapshot and label predicates. It does not report that each page actually documents the field, that its text supports the assigned semantics, or that semantics were independently judged. “Per client” also overstates the counter if it can count a surface more than once or reuse one page/snapshot across surfaces; require distinct, URL-bound pages and exactly one row per surface. “Documents” requires more than a token or a mechanically copied example: require located page evidence establishing that the field is documented, as assessed by named non-author review. The plan's (f) text itself distinguishes a lexical ground token from reviewer judgment, but this is not translated into the headline.

Bounded wording proposed for pre-data addendum (must be adopted before freeze, not silently post-hoc):

> “The counter reports **form-backed records** for up to six named source surfaces, not verified semantics: each counted record has a distinct page snapshot bound to its cited URL and a quote/token/pin chain satisfying the frozen form predicates. The `allowed-tools` meaning for each surface is a separate claim only where named non-author reviewers have confirmed from that page that it documents the field and that the entry's grant label is supported. Script-only world: M of 6 form records (measured on the frozen counter); its score is form-level, not semantic evidence.”

If the intent remains a depth claim, add review state as a conjunct/output: a counted surface must have a cited non-author review for that surface, recorded against the exact snapshot hash, confirming both documentation of the field and the grant label. Alternatively, make the headline explicitly “form-backed records” and leave semantic claims separately bounded to reviewed surfaces. The smallest safe correction is the latter plus distinct URL-bound snapshots and quote/page uniqueness; it avoids pretending lexical counters can decide entailment.
