## My own reading (declared, not a decision)

E05's patch is the only one that composes order-independently with the separately published E04
patch, and its marker is not SOURCES-row-bound (Addendum 2 asks for line scope generally). Y01's is
narrower and leaves one line of cap headroom. If the L1 keeps E04 as its own patch, option B is the
lower-risk path; if the L1 prefers zero counter churn, option A is valid but supersedes E04's patch.
Either way the two counter pin lines above must name the bytes that actually land.
