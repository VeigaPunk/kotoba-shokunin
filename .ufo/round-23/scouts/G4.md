   **current and correct**; the `14` inside the awk output it quotes is labelled
   "was" (pre-round-12) and is not a stale claim. SHONEN.md is 50 lines.
2. **Budget holds:** `SKILL.md` 516 lines against the bound carried by the box at
   `SKILL.md:244` (`File ≤ 600 lines`), enforced as `trigger.line_budget`
   (live: `evaluated 516 within budget 600`). Headroom = **84 lines**.
3. **Cost of the planned 2-line insert (measured, not estimated): 516 → 518,
   headroom 84 → 82.** Round 22 bought 85 lines (601 → 516); the guard bullet
   spends 2 of them. P1.md's inferred conflict ("the pin-guard landing and the
   line-budget repair pull in opposite directions / claim B dies", P1.md:96,
   :209) is **stale**: it costed the insert against the `b16300f` tree (601 lines).
   No line conflict remains at `5a33c3e`.
4. **The sharper cost is not lines, it is landing order.** The insert cites
   `verifiers/check_substrate_pins.py`; `trigger.citation_resolves` resolves every
   repo-rooted token, so a doc-first landing exits **1** naming the inserting line
   (P1). The bullet and the script must land in the **same commit** — a coupling
   P1's fragment framed only as a line problem.
5. **Cheaper variants are available and measured:** folded clause on a new line =
   **+1** (P3); clause appended to the existing §Sources bullet at line 462 =
   **+0** (P4, 104 chars, inside the section's existing 15–106 char wrap band).
   The landing round may pay 0, 1 or 2 lines; the 2-line form is the most
   readable, the 0-line form the most budget-preserving.
6. **Box-map discipline:** prose-only inserts keep the map green (P2/P4:
   `41 boxes mapped`); any insert that adds, removes or rewords a `- [ ]` line
   reds `checklist.box_map_complete` with `unmapped box at line 498` (P5). The
   instrument bullet must be prose, not a checklist box, or the round must
   re-baseline `verifiers/checklist-baseline.json` in the same commit.
7. **Corpus side-budget is consistent:** `corpus.index.tracked_files` is pinned
   `expect: 52` (`verifiers/corpus-baseline.json:13-16`) and observed live as 52
   (`9/9 checks pass`), so claim P's planned re-record `52 → 53` is arithmetically
   right **iff exactly one new tracked corpus file lands and is indexed in

8. **SHONEN.md is unenforced prose.** `grep -rn "SHONEN"` over `*.py`/`*.sh`/`*.json`
