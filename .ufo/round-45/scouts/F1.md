# Round 45 F1 — closed-census fixture coverage audit

## Scope and rule

This is a static coverage audit of the **15 arms classified `closed` by the saved R41 V10 census**, not a new census replay or a production closure claim. A durable comparator fixture means a `case.json` that is tracked at the pinned HEAD and run by the comparator's fixture battery. I distinguish a case for the same guard family from an arm-shaped case: a related fixture, an inline self-test assertion, or a normal control does not become an exact durable payload case.

The audit does not claim that all 15 closed arms have been independently replayed by F1. R41's report records that its own replay reproduced 46/46 outcomes and that the saved classification contains 15 closed arms. H01's separate R45 replay is recorded in `LOG.md`; it is not F1 work and does not change the R41 baseline under review here.

### Coverage gaps

Under the arm-shaped payload rule above, **11 of the 15 R41-closed arms lack a dedicated `case.json`**: `f3-consistent-value-disagreement`, `f3-rows-missing`, `live-mode-equal-with-independent-drift`, `r1-f3-prime-scope-file-honest-cwd-control`, `results-dotted-map-divergent`, `row-map-divergent`, `scope-uncommitted-extra-row`, `scoped-out-record-without-map`, `status-primary-unlanded`, `t1-duplicate-id-divergent-copy`, and `w1-stringified-rows`. The nearest related fixtures and inline assertions are shown in the crosswalk; they should not be reported as exact payload fixtures. The four clearest mechanism-specific holes, for which the nearest case still exercises a different structural condition, are the missing-row, honest-CWD scope-resolution, duplicate-ID, and stringified-row arms.

Four arm-to-case associations are represented by three case files: `expected-launder`, `rows-shadow`, and `checks-shadow` (the latter is relevant to two checks-channel arms). This is a bounded case inventory, not a claim that the battery is an exhaustive census of all accepted JSON shapes.
