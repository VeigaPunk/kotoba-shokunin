## Disposition and limits

- Confirmed: in-scope values and modes can separate the headline from strict agreement; `positive` can accept a strict mismatch, increasing adjudications while preserving a green protocol headline. Live `expected` exclusively controls the demonstrated secondary change.
- Confirmed residuals: same-value duplicate rows can shadow status; status/state coercion and duplicate status shadow can suppress provisional handling; live mode and verdict metadata are not reconciled across row channels; `expected_value` conflict with `expected` is not rejected; several probe/scope-evidence decorations can vary without changing primary outputs. The 11 green-arm cases and two fresh edge cases remain reportable open doors, not closures.
- Scope-out value/key laundering, invalid IDs, channel divergence/membership, missing derived values, and invalid scope declarations represented in the 14 open-arm transitions were rejected by exit 2 on the pinned bytes.
- No comparator, scope file, floor, registry, mission/axis/corpus protocol, or fixture source was edited. No assertion-floor, corpus, standard gate, landing rerun, or SHONEN check was performed in this reporting seat. The results do not establish round-45 axis movement or final closure.
