## Proposal

`drafts/p03/proposal.patch` adds a top-level schema gate to `parse_independent`:

- Preserve the row channels (`results`, `rows`, `checks`) and the legacy single-map forms (`expected`, `re_derived`, `observations`, or a flat dotted-id map).
- Reject competing map aliases, and reject any top-level value-map override alongside row channels. Thus a clean row channel cannot silently shadow an `expected` map.
- Reject uncontracted top-level keys, including `metadata`, `overrides`, and arbitrary future keys, with a named `CompareError` (CLI exit 2).
- Explicitly allow the producer's current auxiliary fields: `landed`, `unlanded`, `alternatives` (ignored for join values), plus the scoped-out declaration/probe fields. The live producer emits all of these, so rejecting every non-row key would break the honest payload.

The two `alternatives` tests prove it remains non-contractual rather than a join channel. The patch deliberately does **not** validate the truth of `landed`/`unlanded` or `alternatives`; contradictions in those diagnostic fields remain an explicit boundary, not a claim that their summary channel was reconciled.
