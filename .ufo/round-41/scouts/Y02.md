The explicit `NOT DERIVED` F-2 arm is closed, but an expected-only full copy is still sufficient to pass.

## Census assessment and decision

The 46-arm V10 matrix is useful corroboration, but it is not evidence that the hunt found no unlisted door: it contains the accepted cases above, including the new expected-only full copy and 28/28 wrapper variants. The Y02 and V10 drafts also show that changing payload shape, field precedence, scope-file path, and probe target exposes distinct surfaces. There is no single complete, reviewed census with an exhaustive payload-channel inventory and a clean held-out hunt. Therefore C1’s completeness condition is unmet even though the comparator self-test and honest join pass. **Depth remains 9; hold.**
