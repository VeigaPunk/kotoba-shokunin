It selects `status` with `record.get("status", record.get("state"))` (`:339`), so a present `status` takes precedence and its sibling `state` is not compared. The top-level `overrides`, `metadata`, `alternatives`, and arbitrary added key are outside the parsed channel set and are not compared to the join.
