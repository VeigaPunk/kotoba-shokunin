### Open door mechanism

R1’s T1 duplicate-value arm does not cover this equal-value/status-split case.

The narrow fix target is to reject duplicate IDs in every row channel, or reconcile every per-record metadata field that affects the verdict before deduplication. At minimum, duplicate status/state disagreement must be named and fail closed, regardless of ordering.
