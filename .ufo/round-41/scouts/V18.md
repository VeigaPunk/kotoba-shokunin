## Limitations and disclosure

The six-process wave reproduces Q1's concurrency proxy, but not the exact concurrent sibling writes associated with V3's event. It therefore supports WATCH / not currently reproducible, not a proof that the original condition is impossible. My first timeout probe used 50 ms and could not distinguish a deliberate hang from ordinary startup latency; I discarded that reading and re-ran with a marker-writing 60 s sleeper and 10 s limit. The cited timeout evidence is only the latter. No tracked files were changed by this seat; the guard and registry modifications visible in `git status` were V16's concurrent landing. All V18 artifacts are under `.ufo/round-41/drafts/v18/`; no git state was mutated.
