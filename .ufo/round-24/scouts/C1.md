## Findings

### E1 — Claim E names the wrong SKILL.md site, and it is a record, not a stale claim

Claim E's extra item is "SKILL.md '27/27'". The only `27/27` in `SKILL.md` is `:456`,
inside the round-9 cache paragraph and explicitly dated ("**round 10** re-verified by
`verifiers/verify_harness_claims.py`, 27/27 checks"). Round-23 scout F4 classified exactly
this line as a dated record to **keep** (`.ufo/round-23/scouts/F4.md:79`). The genuinely
stale present-tense sites are `SKILL.md:109` (`**27 checks**`, under the imperative
"Run the verifier instead of re-typing the recipe") and `SKILL.md:461` ("the authority for
every harness number in this file") — **both unnamed in claim E**. Live authority: command 1
prints `28/28`. So the named item is at best a no-op and at worst a record rewrite, while
the two real sites are left out.

### E2 — Claim E's "SCORECARD coverage row '4 checks'" is a dated record in both places

`grep -n "4 checks" SCORECARD.md` returns two sites: `:30`, inside a span the cell itself
prefixes **"Pre-round-15 evidence:"**, and `:68`, the round-13 round-table row ("@ round-13
worktree"). Neither is present-tense; the same `:30` cell already carries the current
"coverage machine gate `verify_surfaces.py` 7/7 unaffected" (commands 8–9). Live is 7 checks /
75 assertions (commands 3–4). There is **no present-tense `4 checks` row** in SCORECARD. The
item is mis-targeted in exactly the same way as E1, and for the same reason: F4's
present-tense/dated classification (`.ufo/round-23/scouts/F4.md:70-85`) was not carried into
the claim.

### E3 — Claim E drops `corpus/README.md:49`, the third occurrence of the stale anchor

The stale `PROTOCOL.md:34-39` appears three times in tracked files: `SHONEN.md:7`, `SHONEN.md:46`,
and **`corpus/README.md:49`** (command 10). Claim E lists SHONEN only. E4.md itself flags
README:49 as required by the "corrections sweep every mention" rule
(`.ufo/round-24/scouts/E4.md` finding F3 / defect 4). Landing E as worded leaves the sweep
incomplete, and E's own `after` ("each corrected value re-measures live") cannot detect the
omission because no counting command sweeps for the string.

### E4 — One census edit sits inside the sha256-pinned frozen block, and the claim does not say so

The stale "seven code-shape rules" occurs at census `:28`, `:287` **and `:337`**; `:337` is

claim E's `counting_command` omits the frozen-block re-check. The claim's falsifier ("any
rewrite beyond the minimal erratum") gives the landing lane no test for the property that
actually matters here.

### E5 — The round-12 item is a record rewrite under a rule the claim does not cite

The repo rule is at `corpus/knowledge/checklist-execution-round15.md:76`: "a filed report is a

substitution. The two are different moves with different risk; the claim must name which.
(No gate reads that file — command 15 — so the risk is documentary, not mechanical.)

### H1 — Claim H names the mechanism its own falsifier forbids

Claim H's text names F8's **Fix B**: "the fixture-resolution fallback (:386-392) must locate
fixtures relative to the script, not the cwd-or-git heuristic" (`find_repo_root` `return cur`
→ `return DEFAULT_REPO`). H's `after` and `falsifier` demand **"live tree byte-identical
behavior"** and "live-gate behavior change" as a kill condition — which is F8's **Fix A** (pin
`--repo` at the two fixture argv sites, `:1341` + `:1555`). Measured:

- **A** (2 hunks): archive self-test `0/1504` rc 0 (cmd 21); live default gate and `--self-test`
  untouched — A never enters the non-self-test path.
- **B** (1 line + docstring): archive self-test `0/1504` rc 0 (cmd 22), but it **changes the
  live CLI**: a foreign `--skill <dir>/SKILL.md` in a `.git`-less tree goes from
  `FAIL governance.citation_resolves … unreadable` to `N/A governance` (commands 23–24). B is a
  no-op on the live tree only because `.git` exists (cmd 25).
- B additionally falsifies three in-file statements that say the fallback is the target's own
  directory: `:142`, `:180-181`, `:1707-1708` (cmd 26). F8 says B must land *with* those three
  edits or it creates doc drift; claim H does not mention them.

So H as worded is self-contradictory: it lands B and then asserts A's invariance. The
defensible landing is **A** (byte-invariant), with B recorded as an optional CLI-scope
follow-up carrying its three docstring edits.

### H2 — "LANDS ONLY IF the /tmp simulations are unanimous" is not operationalized

The condition names no sim set, no count, and no observable. As written, one green archive run
satisfies "unanimous". A defensible form: A and B each green the archive *and* the
`tar --exclude=.git` twin; A byte-invariant on the live tree; the red probes still flip. F8 ran
four layouts (`.ufo/round-23/scouts/F8.md` commands 8–10, 16–18); the claim lost the shape.

### H3 — Hidden trade: H falsifies five recorded sha pins on the most delicate instrument

`verify_skill_checklist.py`'s sha `02ccf1b310f7910502f7938d24eb1cfcabe2c521ec42b7ce6420f1869af8df27`
is pinned at `corpus/knowledge/checklist-execution-round17.md:221,245`, `LOG.md:850,992`, and
`corpus/knowledge/sibling-citation-routing-round16.json:9` (command 17). Any H landing
supersedes those pins — a documentary debt created by an operability move, unacknowledged in
claim H. "Live behavior byte-invariant" is not the same as "no recorded pin falsified": the
pins are on the file's bytes, not its behavior. This is not a blocker, but it belongs in the
claim, and it is the same defect class E exists to repair — so landing H without it makes the
round's two claims pull in opposite directions.

### H4 — "0/1504" is the wrong observable on its own

The assertion total is **1504 in both layouts** (cmd 18: 108 failures over 1504; cmd 19: 0 over
1504). So "0/1504" tests the failure count only. F8's own recommendation is to assert the
literal `1504` as an anti-shrink tripwire (the P7 class: a guard that goes green by dropping
assertions). Claim H's `counting_command` prints the count but its falsifier never names the
shrink case, so a fix that silently drops assertions would pass H's falsifier.

### Falsifier quality

- **E's falsifier** ("any corrected value that fails to re-measure, any gate flipping, any
  rewrite beyond the minimal erratum") is well-shaped but has a hole: it cannot catch a
  *wrongly-targeted* correction. "Fixing" `SKILL.md:456` to `28/28` re-measures cleanly and
  flips no gate, yet falsifies a dated record. Add: "every edited site was classified
  present-tense first; no dated/record site is rewritten."
- **H's falsifier** is concrete and strong (four named flips) — but it is attached to the wrong
  fix, so it would pass B-with-its-docstring-edits while the claim text claims A's invariance.

**Verdicts.** **E: HOLD — not minimal and mis-targeted.** Two of its named items are dated
records (defects 1–2), two required sites are missing (defects 3, 4), and one item is
