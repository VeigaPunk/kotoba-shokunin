### 3. Under the protocol contract the audit is 5 of 23, and that is expected

`Landed changes` + `Before → after measurements` + `SHONEN`. Rounds 3–13 pass the

order' .ufo/prompts/*.md` returns **exactly one kotoba prompt** (`kotoba-round-16.md`);

is: *5 of 23 reports are contract-conformant; 13 of 23 are complete reports under the
H2 bar; 10 are incomplete or absent, unchanged since round 21.* Any future count that
quotes a single number without naming the bar will be re-audited (this is exactly how
round 21's "64/64" and round 22's "62 lanes" earned their re-audits).

## Limits (what I did NOT verify)

- **Quality beyond the three criteria.** A report can exist, carry SHONEN, exceed
  500 B and still be wrong; S11 judged presence, not truth (except where it
  cross-checked the round-23 report's own claims, items 1 and D4-D5).
- **SHONEN presence is a heading-regex test plus a full heading dump for the failing
  set.** A SHONEN section rendered as prose inside another section counts as absent.
- **The >500 B bar is inherited from H2, not derived.** It is a proxy: round-19's
  3166 B and round-16's 9700 B are judged equally "substantive".
- **Rounds 1–19 byte sizes are live-now values**, not values at their landing commits;
  no drift is observable against H2's records (identical for every round it measured),
  but that is agreement between two live reads, not a historical reconstruction.
- **Did not audit non-kotoba lanes**, the fleet `AGGREGATE.md` / `fleet-index-2026.md`,
  or whether the shared root's round numbering is fleet-global.
- **Did not reconstruct the true dispatched seat counts** from driver logs, sessions
  or wave registries — D5 compares the report's number only against the persisted
  fragment tree and `claims.json`'s own plan value, exactly as H2 and scout G3 had to.
- **No mutation probe was needed or run** (pure stat/grep/git-object audit). Live tree
  untouched: `git status --porcelain` shows only the pre-existing `?? .ufo/round-24/`
  and the other lane's `?? .ufo/reports/round-24/`; the only writes were
