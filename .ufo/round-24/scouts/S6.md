## Limits (what you did NOT verify)

- **The index tokenizer gap is characterised, not adjudicated.** I established that 69 raw / 67 author / 69 independent is three definitions over the same 69 cells and named the two dropped tokens (F4/D9), but I did not decide which definition the errata should record — that is the round's editorial call, and the two dropped tokens are directory-shaped, so neither is a corpus file.
- **No independent re-derivation of the harness, surfaces, checklist or pin instruments.** I ran them (runs 23–28) and read their verdict lines, but only the corpus counts were re-derived from spec by me. The 28-vs-27, 38-vs-41 and 6-vs-7 counts are read off the tools' own output, so a bug inside those tools would reproduce its own number.
- **`--self-test` falsification not attempted.** I ran both self-tests green (runs 9/10) but did not plant a fault to prove they can go red. The committed fault harness (runs 21/22) does that for the corpus pair and passed; the harness/surfaces/checklist self-tests are taken on their exit code alone.
- **Historical records not adjudicated.** Whether `SCORECARD.md:28/:65` (27 checks) are defects depends on the per-round-record claim-scope rule, which is a protocol decision. I applied the reading that living docs (`harness-verifier-round10.md`, `SKILL.md`, `README.md` rows, `SHONEN.md`) are stale and dated round rows are history, and said so at item 6 rather than silently choosing.
- **Remote/other-host behaviour not measured.** All host-anchored figures are measured on this host only; the masked-`.ufo` probe (run 20) is the closest available proxy and was run under `unshare` on the live tree without writing to it.
- **`/tmp` did not survive the run.** The extractor, both clones and the raw JSON were destroyed by tmpfs pressure mid-run and rebuilt; the numbers in this fragment are from the post-wipe re-runs, which reproduced the pre-wipe values exactly (checked where the pre-wipe output survived).
- **`corpus/knowledge/instruction-surface-census-round13.md`'s round-22 byte-frozen `SKILL.md` block** (lines ~328+) is under a sha256 preservation gate; I did not test whether the errata sites D1–D3 fall inside or outside that frozen span — round-23 scout F3 already found one such collision at census `:337` and any fix must respect it.
