## Findings

**F1 — the literal grep is clean, 12/12.** Command 10: not one of the twelve stale
literals (`"27 checks"`, `"27/27"`, `"10/19"`, `"34-39"`, `"4 checks"`,
`"72 assertions"`, `"41 \`extra row\`"`, `"9 declared"`, `"seven code-shape"`,
`"Trigger-fidelity checklist: 14"`, `"seven named rules"`, `"nine declared"`) occurs
anywhere in `verifiers/*.py`, `verifiers/*.sh` or `verifiers/*.json`. The verifiers
never quote the strings they would have to match.

**F2 — the census: the parser's section and the errata's section are disjoint.**
`verify_surfaces.py:169` anchors on `^##\s+Class inventory` and `:452-470` runs that
section to the line before the next `^## `. Measured at HEAD (command 12): the
parsed section is **lines 64–239**. The E1/E7a errata sites are at **:28, :272,
:287, :288, :293, :337** — every one of them **outside** 64–239. The only other
census reads are `inventory_class_ids` / `inventory_rows` (`:1018-1020`), both fed
by the same section. Consequence: the census errata cannot move any of the seven
`surfaces.*` checks.

Corollary (worth carrying to the claim owner, it is a *landing hazard* not an
interlock): the census `:337` site sits inside the round-22 absorbed block, whose
91-line body is pinned at `e046a025…` and claimed as a substring. No live gate
enforces that pin (command 19: zero `e046a025` hits under `verifiers/`), so the pin
is documentary — but it is a *self-imposed* constraint of the census file, and
scout F3 already drafted the addendum-outside-the-window handling for it. I verified
the property survives my edit placement (command 33: the frozen body still hashes
exactly and remains a substring, now at lines 333–423).

**F3 — SHONEN.md is read by nothing at all.** Command 13: `grep -rn "SHONEN"
verifiers/` → exit 1, no match, in all six scripts. The E4 anchor (`:7`, `:46`) and
the embedded awk output (`:15`) are therefore ungated by construction — which is
exactly what round-23 scout F5's F3 concluded independently ("no file"), and it is
the *reason* the stale quote survived five SKILL.md revisions.

**F4 — the checklist reads paths, never counts — proven both ways.** The checklist
does read `SKILL.md`, `SCORECARD.md` and `LOG.md`, so the E7 sites needed a probe,
not just a grep. Commands 30/31 settle it as a controlled pair on the *same
paragraphs*:

- probeC changes only numbers (`28→99` in SKILL.md `:109`; `7 checks, live 7/7 →
  99 checks, live 99/99` in SCORECARD) → output **identical** to the unmodified
  farmB. The counts are invisible.
- probeD changes only path tokens in the same paragraphs (`verifiers/baseline.json`
  → `verifiers/NOPE.json`; `verifiers/surfaces-baseline.json` →
  `verifiers/NOPE-baseline.json`) → `FAIL trigger.citation_resolves … line 108`,
  governance `+4 more`. The paths are visible.

This matches the code: `citation_subjects()` (`:1159-1215`) keeps a token only when
its normalized form starts with a `CITATION_ROOTS` prefix (`:342`), and
`citation_resolve()` (`:1117`) is an existence test. A count phrase is not
path-shaped, so it never enters the subject set. Note the E7b SCORECARD `"4 checks"`
sites are also *inside historical cells* ("Pre-round-15 evidence:", the round-13
row) whose sibling text carries resolvable path tokens; probeD shows the checker
would fire on those tokens if they were broken, and probeC shows the numbers in the
same cells are inert.

**F5 — the corpus verifier is content-blind for these edits.** Command 18:
`verify_corpus_integrity.py` contains **no** `sha256`/`hashlib`/`mtime` manifest. It
counts tracked files, report pointers (`UFO_POINTER_RE`) and backticked commit
tokens (`COMMIT_RE`). The A/B confirms it executably: its output is byte-identical
between farmA and farmB (command 24 family). Round-23 scout F3 (`:31-40`) and F6
(`§5`) reach the same conclusion for their own drafts; my measurement extends it to
all seven sites at once, including the three edits inside `corpus/knowledge/`.

**F6 — the A/B is clean modulo one artifact, and that artifact is not the errata.**
All six gates exit identically on farmA vs farmB and every output line matches after
normalizing the temp path (command 24). The single live FAIL inside the temp farm —
`governance.citation_resolves`, failing 4 sibling tokens such as `../MISSION.md` and
`../SCORECARD.md` — is a farm-location artifact: command 25 shows the **same 4
tokens** failing in both farms, and the un-suffixed `live` copy in the same farm
shape passes. The live repo passes 10/10 with `evaluated 198 … 198 token(s)
resolve` (command 26). So the FAIL is caused by `/tmp/e6lane/farmX/kotoba-shokunin`
not sitting under the real fleet root, not by any errata.

**F7 — the checkers are non-vacuous, so "no interlock" is not "no check".**
Commands 29/31 are the positive controls: the surfaces verifier fires on a rewritten
**inventory literal cell** (`literal_parity`, exit 1), and the checklist fires on a
broken **path token** in the very paragraphs the E7 counts live in (exit 1). And
command 27 is the negative control that isolates the *section* boundary: corrupting
the opencode count `5 → 999` **in the errata region** leaves the surfaces output
byte-identical. Together they show the boundary is the `## Class inventory` heading,
not file-level blindness.

## Defects named (each: path:line + evidence)

**No gate-interlock defect found.** For completeness, the two things a landing lane
should treat as constraints rather than interlocks:

1. **`corpus/knowledge/instruction-surface-census-round13.md:337` is inside the
   round-22 absorbed block** (`:332-422` at HEAD; the 91-line body pinned at
   `e046a025…`). No live check enforces the pin (command 19), so this is **not** an
   interlock — but it is a documentary self-constraint the errata must respect:
   in-place editing at `:337` voids the file's own byte/substring claim. Verified
   satisfiable: with my edit placement (no in-place change to the block), the body
   still hashes to `e046a0252cc96f173d148b419b7856e23fe789659cf02bc5b3237e8da26114f5`
   and is still a substring (command 33). Handled by the pre-block addendum F3
   drafted.
2. **`SCORECARD.md:30` and `:68` carry the "4 checks" figure inside historical
   cells that also carry resolvable path tokens** (`verifiers/verify_surfaces.py`,
   `verifiers/surfaces-baseline.json`, `corpus/knowledge/…`). The numbers are inert
   (probeC) but the paths in the same cells are load-bearing (probeD) — a landing
   lane that rewrites a whole cell must keep those tokens resolvable.

Neither is an interlock; both are recorded so the merge does not create one.
