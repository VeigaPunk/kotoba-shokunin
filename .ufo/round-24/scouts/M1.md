## Findings

**F1 — the H defect reproduces exactly, and its cause is one line.** A `git archive HEAD` extract
(command 3) gives `108 failure(s) over 1504 assertions`, exit 1 — byte-identical to scout F8's
round-22 measurement. The 108 decompose with no remainder: **79** are
`governance.citation_resolves: unreadable governance file(s): LOG.md ([Errno 2] …)` (69 distinct
fixture cases), **28** are that case's exit-code assertion flipping (the case's *only* failing check
is governance), and **1** is `command-output-flag`'s extra assertion. Every one traces to
`find_repo_root` (`verifiers/verify_skill_checklist.py:386-392`): with no `.git` marker it returns
`cur` — the *fixture directory* `verifiers/fixtures/checklist/<case>/` — so `--repo` becomes that
directory, the governance check reads `LOG.md`/`SCORECARD.md` beside the fixture, fails
"unreadable", and the whole self-test cascades.

**F2 — a three-line fallback fixes it, and the live gate is byte-invariant.** Appending a second
ancestor walk that accepts `verifiers/checklist-baseline.json` as the repo marker, patched **in
place at one identical absolute path**, takes the gitless export from `108/1504` (rc 1) to
`0/1504` (rc 0) with the **assertion count unchanged at 1504**, while the live-gate output diff is
**empty** (command 6). Placed in a faithful fleet mirror (every fleet-root entry symlinked, `..`
surface identical), the patched clone's full gate diffs **IDENTICAL** to live (command 15) — so the
"byte-unchanged" half of claim H is verified, not assumed. Draft patch:
`/tmp/m1h24/S/verifiers/verify_skill_checklist.py` (and `/tmp/m1h24/export_patched/`).

**F2b — attribution: `find_repo_root` is the mechanism, the self-test's argv is the cause.**
Sibling scout `H1.md` independently reached the same line and makes a distinction worth keeping:
`self_test()`'s `run()` (`:1341-1348`) builds fixture argv with `--skill`/`--map` but **no
`--repo`**, so `main()` (`:1752-1753`) falls through to `find_repo_root` and the fallback
(`else cur`) does the damage. H1's fix adds `--repo str(DEFAULT_REPO)` to the two fixture argv
sites — narrower and self-test-local, and it cannot touch the live path at all. My F2 patch fixes
the shared helper instead. **Both were measured red→green on gitless extracts at a fixed 1504
assertions; neither was compared against the other head-to-head** (H1 measured its own; I measured
mine). If the round lands only one, the `--repo` form is the smaller blast radius and is the
recommended default; the helper form additionally protects any future `.git`-less consumer of the
live gate. This is a note, not a verdict — the H-lane owner should pick one and re-measure.

**F3 — the red-probe battery is intact on the patched tree.** In the patched gitless export a
reworded box still FAILs (command 10) and the self-test's own tamper fixtures
(`map-box-reworded`, `map-missing-entry`, `map-extra-entry`, `map-dup-box`, `map-dup-id`,
`map-unknown-executed`, `map-no-executed-entry`, `map-annotation-mismatch`,
`map-executed-undisclosed`, `map-dead-delegated`, `map-file-mismatch`, `marker-checked`,
`marker-near-miss` — all present under `verifiers/fixtures/checklist/`) still assert their FAILs,
since the export self-test is 0/1504 with them in the run. Non-vacuity is preserved.

**F4 — the gate's red surface is complete and message-precise.** 9 box-side and 8 map-side tampers
flip; the two non-flips are *correct* (T9 re-wrap is hash-invariant by the pinned normalization
rule; the two rc-3 map tampers are schema-gate failures, not check failures). No tamper produced a
traceback, and every FAIL names the line, the section, or the sha256 prefix. Both surfaces report
`evaluated` truthfully (40/41/42 track the live box count).

**F5 — the drafted claim-E errata move no gate.** All five edits (SKILL.md `27 checks` ×3;
SCORECARD `4 checks`→`7`, `28 assertions`→`75`, `live 4/4`→`7/7`, `41 entries`→`38`) applied to a
`cp -a` copy leave the 10-check gate output with **0 diff lines**, rc 0 (command 9). Live
re-measures confirm each new value: `verify_harness_claims.py` → `28/28 checks pass` (rc 0);
`verify_surfaces.py` → 7 PASS (rc 0), `--self-test` → `0 failure(s) over 75 assertions`;
`surfaces-baseline.json` → 38 classes; `verify_corpus_integrity.py` → `9/9 checks pass` (rc 0);
SHONEN's awk re-run → `## Trigger-fidelity checklist: 15;` (E4's F2, independently reproduced);
`corpus/knowledge/independent-verification-round12.md:113` already records W0 `19/19`.

**F6 — the self-test is fixture-only, so it is not a live-file regression gate (named, not a
defect of the patch).** On the T1 copy — whose live `SKILL.md` is tampered — `--self-test` still
reports `0 failure(s) over 1504 assertions` (command 11). By design (`--self-test` exercises
fixtures only), so the *live* gate is the only instrument that catches a live-file tamper. Any
round-24 claim phrased as "the self-test guards SKILL.md" would be false; the drafted claims are
phrased on the live gate, so they are unaffected.

**F7 — shared `/tmp` namespace collision (operability finding for the round).** Mid-run, my first
scratch root `/tmp/h24/m1` was deleted out from under a running battery by a sibling lane (a
second shell reported `No such file or directory` while the same path had existed moments earlier),
which silently invalidated four probe runs. All evidence here was regenerated under `/tmp/m1h24`
and is re-runnable from `bash /tmp/m1h24/run.sh`. Sibling lanes sharing `/tmp/h24/<slice>/` cannot
guarantee their own evidence survives; per-lane roots (as here) do.
