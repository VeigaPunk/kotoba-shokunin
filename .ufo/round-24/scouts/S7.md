## Findings

**F1 — the third witness agrees with both shipped instruments, exactly, live.**
Every load-bearing count is identical across the three implementations at HEAD:
`tracked_files` 53, `index` unindexed/stale/missing 0/0/0, `report_total` 36,
`report_dead` 0, `commit_total` 27, `commit_unresolvable` 0, `commit_nonhash` 0,
and pointer shapes `{absolute 34, tilde 1, relative 1, bare 0}`. Three
independently-written tokenizers landing on the same nine numbers is evidence, not
restatement — the round-12 agreement is *reproducible from the class spec alone*,
by a witness that never read either shipped script. (Raw-population divergences
are definitional, not verdicts: the independent publishes `index_tokens=69` where
the author/third publish `index_patterns=67` — the independent counts tokens
outside the coverage-map section too; both report `outside coverage map = 0`.)

**F2 — the W0 claim is 19/19 in *all three* instruments, with byte-identical dead
sets.** At `d49dfa5`: author `report_dead=19/19`, independent `dead=19/19`, third
`dead=19` — and the **19-site sets are equal as sets** (command 8: both symmetric
differences empty), the four shapes match (`relative 9, absolute 8, tilde 1,
bare 1`), and the single non-hash site (`corpus/README.md:38: round-10`) is the
same in all three. So claim E's item-3 statement "`10/19` → `19/19`" is correct
in both instruments, and the round-12 §7 block's recorded `report_dead=10/19`
(record line 285, and the raw line at `corpus/knowledge/corpus-integrity-round11.md:170`)
**does not reproduce at `d49dfa5` on this host**. The `19/19` is not a lucky
snapshot: it is the same set every instrument derives.

**F3 — the delta is the host-anchored 9, and it is *resolution*, not *grammar*.**
The 19 dead = the doc's 10 sites (the 9 relative `corpus/README.md` rows +
`corpus/lessons/round-3.md:5` bare) **plus** the tilde row
(`corpus/lessons/round-1-aggregator-audit.md:9`) and the 8 lowercase-absolute
`/home/vgpnk/projects/...` rows in the round-1…10 lesson files. `/home/vgpnk/projects`
(lowercase) does not exist on this host, so the 9 pointers that resolved on the
round-12 host now dangle. The token *grammar* is unchanged (`report_total` is still
19, not 10 or 28) — only `report_dead` moved. This is exactly round-23 scout F6's
mechanism, independently re-derived here.

**F4 — the `43 = 37` sentence is a two-commit mash, confirmed by the third witness.**
Record line 59 (`independent-verification-round12.md:59`) equates the author's
`index_patterns` 43 with the independent's `index_tokens` 37 "with 0 tokens outside
the coverage-map section". The third witness measures `index_tokens` **43** at
`633859f` (round-12 landing) and **37** at `7d15407` (pre-landing) — the two numbers
come from *different trees*; the equality is between two tree states, not two
instruments. At HEAD the author reads 67 and the independent 69. (Corroborates
round-23 F6 finding 3; no verdict moves — `index_tokens` is INFO in the
independent.)

**F5 — the third witness is non-vacuous (it can go red).** Command 14: a single
planted dead in-grammar pointer and a single planted non-hash `commit` token each
flip their class (`report_dead` 0→1, `commit_nonhash` 0→1) and the exit 0→1. A
third witness that only ever agreed would be worthless; this one detects the same
two fault classes the round-12 fault harness plants.

**F6 — the two shipped instruments are still the round-12 bytes.** `sha256sum`
(command 18) matches the round-12 doc's pins exactly (`a32da4a4…`, `430fe289…`), so
the agreement in F1/F2 is between *the shipped instruments* and a fresh witness —
no silent instrument drift has occurred since round 12.
