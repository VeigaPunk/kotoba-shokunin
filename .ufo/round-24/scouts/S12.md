- `.ufo/reports/round-23/kotoba.md:46` — "SKILL.md grew 2 lines to 518, box map intact".

Root cause, measured: round 22 landed `4b50d8d` at **516** lines; round 23 landed
`dc4c0c0` at **521** lines. `git diff 4b50d8d dc4c0c0 -- SKILL.md` adds **5** lines

planned-but-not-realized `516 + 2`. Both the delta ("+2 lines") and the resulting
count ("518") are wrong in the round-23 report; `LOG.md:1234` inherits the wrong
count. **Correct value: 521/600.**
