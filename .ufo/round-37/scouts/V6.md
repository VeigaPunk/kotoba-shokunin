## DEFECTS

1. **Builder, C3-blocking at 9ea386da (freshness).** Four probe lines across
   three shapes are classified against cmark (F1). Root cause: `fence_open`/
   `fence_close` cap indentation at three *absolute* columns; CommonMark reads
   the cap inside the container, and the corpus already relies on that reading
   (F5). Fixed in `patched-c` (F3).
2. **Builder battery, note.** `FENCE_SHAPES` has no container shape, so the
   self-test cannot see defect 1; adding S07/S08/S07b (or `patched-c`'s
   `shapes/`) to the battery is what makes the class regress-proof.
3. **Claim text (C3 "before").** The *different-character* closer arm does not
   reproduce red at HEAD: `fence_close`'s predecessor already required
   `run[0] == fence_char` (S03 was green at `f182cd56`). The red arms at HEAD
   are shorter closers, info-string closers, 4-column closers, indented/tab
   fence openers, tilde-info fences, and the container cases — 14 mismatches,
   not the two the claim's wording suggests.
4. **Mine.** Two harness bugs, both found by cross-checking and fixed before
   the numbers above: (a) my first container stack was popped by blank lines, so
   container logic never engaged and two shapes passed by accident;
   (b) `cmark_fenced_content` dropped the last line of unclosed blocks. Limits
   that remain: `spec_rules` still disagrees with cmark on 2 of the 29 §4.5
   spec examples (example 10 and example 15, paragraph-continuation cases), so
   it is a second opinion only — every verdict above rests on cmark.
5. **Process.** Five instrument revisions in ~10 minutes inside one shared
   working tree; a seat that measures without pinning a sha will publish a
   number that never was true of any committed file.
