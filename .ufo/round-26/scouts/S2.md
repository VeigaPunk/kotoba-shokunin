## Findings

- **The guard holds and is honest.** Both binaries match their pins by independent recompute, and
  the pin-independent downstream reader (`verify_harness_claims.py`, which derives omp facts from
  the binary itself) also exits 0 at 28/28 — pins and gate agree, so "3/3 hold" is not a stale
  baseline agreeing with itself.
- **All six filed red probes flip** at their documented codes, text and `--json` agree, two live
  runs are byte-identical, and the archive extract reproduces both — the capability survives
  transport (contrast the round-21 `probe-dir` class). Read-only re-proven by strace and by mtime
  invariance of baseline, both binaries, and the opencode log another gate reads.
- **The check set is narrower than the contract**: `opencode_binary_mtime` is not in
  `EXPECTED_PINS`, so its absence is not `PIN_ABSENT`; if present it is compared (probe → 1). mtime
  tolerance is the pin's own precision (9-digit → 0 ns, 6-digit → 999 ns), so the live ns-exact
  claim is exact, not a lucky window.
