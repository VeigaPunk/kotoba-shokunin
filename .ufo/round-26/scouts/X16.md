bears on round 26's dispatch-time exhaustion record (`.ufo/round-26/claims.json:8`: xai-oauth
`grok-4.7-fast` → `403 out-of-credits` on all 46 reported lanes). Read-only; **no dispatch**, no

## Findings
- **The authoritative local credit signal exists and is fresh.** `~/.cache/ai-usage/status.json`
  (schema v2, `0600`, `ai-usage validate` → `VALID`) was refreshed by the enabled
  `ai-usage.timer` **4 seconds before** `.ufo/round-26/` was created. Its `updated_at` is the
  freshness guard; the journal is the per-provider history.
- **Round 26's `403` is independently corroborated locally.** `grok`: top window
  `SuperGrok Weekly Credits` `used_pct=100` (resets `2026-10-23T05:15:22Z`), `Grok Build (Weekly)`
  `98%`, `GrokChat (Weekly)` `2%`; measured `2026-10-19T06:14:44Z`. This is exactly the
  xai-oauth exhaustion filed at `claims.json:8` — discoverable without any dispatch.
- **Prior rounds corroborate too.** `charm-hyper` `credits.remaining = 0` (round 24's hyper
  `402 out of credits`, `LOG.md:1240`, `.ufo/round-24/claims.json:10`). `opencode-go` per-account
  monthly: accts 2/3/4 at **100%**, 1 at 64%, 5 at 51%, 7 at 20% — three spent keys, the
  round-23 keys-3/4/5 class (`LOG.md:1230`).
- **The aggregate window hides per-key exhaustion.** `opencode-go` top-level reads
  `7-account weekly = 6.8571%` and `5 Hour = 3.7143%` while 3 of 7 keys are at 100% monthly;
  only the `special` "Hottest account" window (100%) and the per-account rows in the waybar
  tooltip expose it.
- **Live capacity at dispatch time:** cursor `4.339%` / other `0.24%`; kimi session `6%`;
  devin weekly `74%`; codex weekly `77%`; zai weekly `81.575%`; google weekly `1.65%`;
  token-plan 2 of 3 accounts at `0` credits, third `23927.81` left (aggregate `95.5689%`).
  The devin fallback round 26 actually took had headroom.
- **No fleet consumer.** Nothing in the fleet tree, the ufo/swarm skills, or the round prompts
  reads the cache; exhaustion is only ever discovered by a failing dispatch and then written into
  `claims.json`/`LOG.md` prose.

## Defects named
1. **No dispatch precheck against the local credit cache.** `~/.cache/ai-usage/status.json` is
   fresh to the minute and already said `grok weekly=100`; a one-line read before the round-26
   dispatch would have named the xai exhaustion before 46 lanes failed. No driver/watchdog/prompt
   references the cache (grep above).
2. **`charm-hyper` reports `status: ok` with `credits.remaining = 0`** — `ai-usage validate` prints
   `charm-hyper: ok credits=0` and exits `VALID`. A precheck keyed on `status` alone would miss a
   fully spent provider; only `credits.remaining` (or a `used_pct == 100` window) discriminates.
3. **Aggregate-only reading misleads.** Top-level `opencode-go weekly=6.8571%` is the number a
   naive precheck would see, while 3 of 7 keys are at 100% monthly — the round-23 failure mode
   (`LOG.md:1230`) is invisible at that level; per-account rows are required.
4. **Cache staleness has no fleet-side guard.** `ai-usage.timer` is enabled and firing on a 5-min
   cadence today, but `updated_at` is the only freshness evidence; a lapsed or failed timer would
   leave a precheck reading stale credits with no error surface.
5. **`kimi` is `status: ok` + `reason: partial_usage` with `snapshot.disabled_credentials = 1`** —
   one kimi credential is disabled and the monthly windows carry no `used_pct`; its headroom is
   only partially observable.

## Limits
Read-only; only `.ufo/round-26/scouts/X16.md` written. No dispatch, and deliberately **no**
`ai-usage probe`/`refresh` (both rewrite the cache) — only cache-reading `validate`/`waybar`, so
every figure is as of `2026-10-19T06:14:56Z`, not a live re-probe. The cache is a third-party
tool's reading of provider APIs; I did not verify its numbers against provider-side truth, and I
did not read credential/token files (secret files are out of scope). Host logs are not a quota
surface: `~/.kimi-code/logs/kimi-code.log` (last write `03:37Z`) and the opencode log carry no
quota records. No claim is made about why `xai-oauth/grok-4.7-fast` specifically returned 403
versus the `SuperGrok Weekly Credits` window that reads 100%.
